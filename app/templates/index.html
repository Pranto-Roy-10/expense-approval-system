Claim lifecycle

One clear path from receipt to reimbursement

Every claim moves through the same statuses, so everyone knows exactly where it stands.

  1. 01 Draft

    Create

    Add line items with date, category, description, amount and currency. Edit freely until ready.

  2. 02 Submitted

    Submit

    The claim locks and lands in the manager's review queue.

  3. 03 Approved

    Approve

    Manager signs off. Claims over 1,000 also require Finance approval.

  4. 04 Paid

    Pay

    Finance marks the claim as paid. The record is final.

Rejected

Rejected claims return to the employee with a comment explaining why. They can edit and resubmit without starting over.

Policy enforcement

Rules checked on the server, not just the form

Expense policy is applied on every request, so it holds no matter how a claim is submitted.

Two-level approval
Claims totalling over 1,000 go to Finance after manager approval.
ManagerFinancePaid
Meal limit
Any meal over 50 requires a note before the claim can be submitted.
No self-approval
No one can approve a claim they submitted, regardless of role.
Itemised claims
Every claim needs at least one line item with date, category, description, amount and currency.
